This Is My Simple Biodata

"Every story has a beginning, and this is a little about mine 🌷"

Hello, everyone! My name is Nur Shamimi Intan Lyana binti Zulhisham. I was born on 07 October 2005 in Taiping, Perak, and now I live in Sungai Buloh, Selangor with my parents, siblings, and my beloved uncle. I am the second child in a family of four siblings, all still studying. My family is strict with me because I am a clumsy and childish person — they worry I might get into accidents or problems. But all of this makes me stronger and shapes who I am today 💖.
